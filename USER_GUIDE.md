# Gangwar User Guide 🎮

Welcome to the streets. This guide will help you navigate the dangerous world of Gangwar and build your criminal empire.

## 🏁 Objective
The goal is simple: **Survive and Dominate**. Accumulate wealth, grow your gang, and eventually take down the legendary Squidie Army to prove you're the king of the streets.

---

## 📍 Key Locations

### 🏙️ City (Main Hub)
This is your primary navigation screen. From here, you can access all other locations in the city. Keep an eye on the status bar for Health, Cash, and the current Day.

### 🗺️ 3D Open World
Experience the city in a fully generated isometric environment.
- **Navigation**: Use the on-screen joystick to explore.
- **Interactions**: Approach buildings to enter them directly from the world map.
- **Random Encounters**: Stay alert for dynamic events that trigger as you wander.

### 🏚️ Crackhouse
The heart of your economy.
- **Trading**: Buy low and sell high. Prices fluctuate daily based on market trends.
- **Substances**: Trade in Weed, Crack, Coke, Ice, Percs, and Pixie Dust.

### 🔫 Gun Shack
Your source for protection and firepower.
- **Arsenal**: From Brass Knuckles to Rocket Launchers.
- **Specialized Ammo**: 
    - **Hollow Point**: Increased damage through tissue destruction.
    - **Exploding Bullets**: Maximum damage via micro-detonations.
- **Armor**: Essential for surviving late-game gang wars.

### 🏦 Bank
Safe-keep your hard-earned cash.
- **Deposit**: Protect your money from being stolen if you lose a fight.
- **Loans**: Take a loan for quick expansion, but beware the **Loan Shark Enforcers** if you don't pay it back!

### 🍺 The Bar
The place for rumors and recruitment.
- **Recruit**: Find new members to join your gang.
- **Intel**: Listen to rumors about upcoming drug busts or price drops.

### 🏪 Pick n Save
General supplies and gang management.
- **Supplies**: Buy food and medical kits to keep your crew alive.
- **Morale**: High morale improves gang performance in combat.

---

## ⚔️ Combat & Survival

### Gang Wars & Brawls
Combat is turn-based and brutal.
- **Attack**: Choose your weapon wisely. Firearm effectiveness depends on ammo.
- **Gang Support**: Your crew will fight alongside you, using the best weapons available in your inventory.
- **Critical Hits**: Enemies can land devastating critical blows—keep your health high!
- **Flee**: Sometimes survival is better than pride. Attempt to escape if the odds are against you.

### Health & Death
- If your health reaches **0**, you lose a life. If all lives are lost, the game ends.
- Use **Percs** or medical supplies from **Pick n Save** to heal during or after combat.

---

## 📈 Tips for Success

1. **Leverage the 3D World**: Exploring the open world can lead to unique encounters and rewards not found in the static menu.
2. **Watch for Trends**: Check the Bar for intel. If a "Bust" is coming, sell your stock immediately before prices tank.
3. **Equip Your Gang**: A large gang is useless without weapons. The combat system automatically distributes your inventory to your members during a fight.
4. **Pay Your Debts**: Don't let your loan go unpaid for too long, or the Loan Shark Enforcers will hunt you down in the streets.

---

## 🛠️ Controls
- **Joystick**: Move character in the 3D Open World.
- **Tap**: Select locations, interact with NPCs, and confirm actions.
- **Back Button**: Returns you to the City Hub or Main Menu.
